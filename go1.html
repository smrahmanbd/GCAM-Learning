Is this go for you?

You need: Nothing. Curiosity about climate and energy, and comfort reading a simple chart.

How it works: Pictures first. Every idea is a diagram you can click and a small explorer where you predict an answer, then check it. No code needed and no maths beyond percentages.

Quick entry check

1. An integrated assessment model is best described as…

It works out what follows from assumptions, consistently. It does not predict or decide.

2. Warming depends mostly on…

Warming tracks cumulative CO2: about 0.45 °C per 1,000 Gt.

3. In 'SSP2-4.5', the 4.5 is…

SSP is the story about society; the number is the radiative forcing in 2100.

All three right? You could skip ahead to Go 2, or use this go as revision.

What you will produce

  • A one-page explanation, in your own words, of what an IAM is and is not
  • Your own reading of one IPCC scenario chart: what the labels mean and what the band shows
  • A first walk through GCAM: its regions, markets, land, water and climate model
