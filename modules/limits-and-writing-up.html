Research brief

In this module you will

  • List what GCAM transport cannot see, and how to state those limits
  • Identify equity and ethical choices hidden in scenario assumptions
  • Describe your GCAM version and changes following the community guidelines
  • Report results in IAMC variables and publish a package that rebuilds every figure
Spoken overview
Read the transcript

Every model has blind spots, and a good study names them. GCAM transport does not track the vehicle fleet, has limited behavioural detail, treats aviation and shipping fuels as one pool of liquids, and has no explicit infrastructure such as charging networks. Say so, and say how each limit could bias your answer. Scenarios also carry ethical choices: whose travel grows, who pays for the transition, and whether results rely on carbon removal later in the century. Make those choices visible. When you write up, follow the GCAM community guidelines: state the exact version, every change you made to inputs and code, and where to find them. Report results using standard variable names from the Integrated Assessment Modeling Consortium, so others can compare them with other models. Finally, publish a reproducible package: the GCAM version, your add-on files, configuration and batch files, queries, analysis scripts and a short readme. If someone else cannot rebuild your figures, the study is not finished.

لكل نموذج نقاط عمياء، والدراسة الجيدة تسمّيها. فقطاع النقل في GCAM لا يتتبع أسطول المركبات، وتفاصيله السلوكية محدودة، ويعامل وقود الطيران والشحن البحري كمخزون واحد من الوقود السائل، ولا يمثل البنية التحتية صراحةً مثل شبكات الشحن. اذكر ذلك، واذكر كيف يمكن لكل قيد أن يُحيز إجابتك. وتحمل السيناريوهات أيضًا خيارات أخلاقية: سفرُ مَن ينمو، ومَن يدفع تكلفة التحول، وهل تعتمد النتائج على إزالة الكربون في أواخر القرن. اجعل هذه الخيارات ظاهرة. وعند الكتابة، اتبع إرشادات مجتمع GCAM: اذكر الإصدار بدقة، وكل تغيير أجريته على المدخلات والشيفرة، ومكان العثور عليه. واعرض النتائج بأسماء المتغيرات القياسية لاتحاد نمذجة التقييم المتكامل، حتى يتمكن الآخرون من مقارنتها بالنماذج الأخرى. وأخيرًا، انشر حزمة قابلة لإعادة الإنتاج: إصدار النموذج، وملفاتك الإضافية، وملفات الإعدادات والتشغيل الدفعي، والاستعلامات، وبرامج التحليل، وملف تعريفي قصير. فإن لم يستطع غيرك إعادة بناء أشكالك، فالدراسة لم تكتمل بعد.

Why this matters

A net-zero transport pathway with 80 % zero-emission car sales can still leave aviation, shipping and heavy freight emitting tens of megatonnes a year, balanced by direct air capture that does not yet exist at scale. The model will report it as "net zero". Whether that is a plan or a gamble is not a question the model answers — and your paper has to say so.

Ethics: whose future, whose cost?

  • Reliance on carbon removal. Net-zero transport in GCAM-KSA leans on direct air capture for aviation and shipping residuals (Qiu et al. 2024). Report gross and net emissions separately, and show a case with limited removal.
  • Distribution. A carbon price or fuel-price reform raises the cost of travel for households that cannot switch. GCAM's single representative consumer cannot show who pays; say so and point to studies that can.
  • Oil exporters. For Saudi Arabia, transport decarbonisation interacts with domestic fuel-price reform and export revenues; a cost-minimising pathway is not automatically a socially acceptable one.
  • Authority of numbers. A single scenario figure is easily quoted as a forecast. Show ranges, label scenarios by assumptions, and include "what the model cannot tell us" in the abstract, not only in the appendix.

Describing GCAM in a paper

JGCRI publishes suggested community guidelines for peer-reviewed articles that use GCAM (community-guide.html, checked 23 October 2026). In short:

GuidelineIn your paper
State the version"We use GCAM v9.1 (tag gcam-v9.1)" — never just "GCAM"
Describe the model in a short paragraphmultisector model developed at JGCRI, open source; 32 energy-economy regions, land in more than 300 subregions (396 in the guideline text), 235 water basins; cite JGCRI (2025), GCAM documentation v8.2, doi:10.5281/zenodo.15581183, plus model papers relevant to your sector
Data and code availabilitycite the public repository of the version used, with its DOI
Describe every modificationlist every departure from the public release; for substantial changes, rename the model with an identifier, e.g. "GCAM-<institution>" (as GCAM-KSA does)
Ask and collaboratequestions go to GCAM GitHub Discussions; JGCRI team members may be invited as co-authors

Research note

Two numbers in the guideline. The community guideline says 396 land subregions, while the documentation overview says "more than 300" (the counts differ between pages and versions). Quote the number that matches your version's input files and say where it comes from.

Reporting in the IAMC format

Report results in the IAMC template (model, scenario, region, variable, unit, then one column per year) with standard variable names, so they can be compared with the AR6 and CMIP7 scenario databases and read with pyam. Typical transport variables:

IAMC variableGCAM source
Energy Service|Transportation|Passenger (bn pkm/yr)transport service output by service (trn_pass)
Energy Service|Transportation|Freight (bn tkm/yr)transport service output by service (trn_freight)
Final Energy|Transportation|Electricity / Liquids / Hydrogen / Gases (EJ/yr)transport final energy by fuel
Emissions|CO2|Energy|Demand|Transportation (Mt CO2/yr)CO2 emissions by sector (excluding resource production)
Price|Carbon (US$2010/t CO2)CO2 prices (converted from 1990$/tC)

The reproducible package

Publish (or archive with a DOI, e.g. on Zenodo) a package that lets a reader rebuild every figure: the GCAM version and commit; every add-on XML and configuration or batch file; any gcamdata modification chunk; the query file; the analysis scripts; the extracted results table; and a README with the run log (module 3.1). Large output databases do not need to be shared if the extracted table and the means to rebuild it are.

Pitfall

"Results available on request." For a model that anyone can download, this is no longer acceptable to many journals or reviewers. If a reader cannot rebuild your scenarios from the paper and its archive, the scenarios are not reproducible — however carefully they were run.

# Reporting checklist for a GCAM transport study

Tick every line before submitting a report, thesis chapter or paper.

## A. Model description (GCAM community guidelines, https://jgcri.github.io/gcam-doc/community-guide.html)
- [ ] GCAM version stated ("GCAM v9.1"), with the public repository and the DOI of that release cited.
- [ ] Documentation cited (the guidelines suggest "JGCRI, 2025. GCAM Documentation (Version 8.2)" with its Zenodo DOI; note that the docs describe v8.2 while the release used is 9.x).
- [ ] Standard introductory description of GCAM used, with links to the documentation.
- [ ] Every change from the public model listed (file, element, old and new value, reason). Substantial changes: model renamed "GCAM-<institution>" and the change stated in the opening paragraph.
- [ ] GCAM team members invited as co-authors where their expertise is used (guidelines); questions on data or results raised on GCAM GitHub Discussions.

## B. Transport-specific transparency
- [ ] Nesting and logit exponents used (default from A54.sector.csv and A54.tranSubsector_logit_revised.csv, or changed).
- [ ] Source of vehicle costs and intensities named (OTAQ_trn_data_EMF37.csv overrides UCD_trn_data_CORE.csv for most modes in 9.1).
- [ ] Units stated: pkm/tkm in billions, costs 1975$/vkm, intensities EJ per billion vkm, carbon prices 1990$/tC (converted for the reader, e.g. to 2024$/tCO2, with the deflator named).
- [ ] Share-weight changes reported as assumptions, with the reason.
- [ ] Statement that GCAM has no vehicle stock: "sales shares" are new-vintage output shares.
- [ ] Value-of-time settings (time-value multipliers, speeds, wage definition) if mode shift matters to the result.

## C. Results reporting (IAMC-style variable names)
Report regional results in the IAMC template format (model, scenario, region, variable, unit, years) so they can be compared with other models. Names below follow the IAMC/AR6 template convention; check them against the current IAMC common definitions (https://github.com/IAMconsortium/common-definitions) before submission.
| Variable | Unit | GCAM source query |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Final Energy\|Transportation | EJ/yr | transport final energy by fuel (sum) |
| Final Energy\|Transportation\|Liquids | EJ/yr | ... fuel = refined liquids enduse |
| Final Energy\|Transportation\|Electricity | EJ/yr | ... fuel = elect_td_trn |
| Final Energy\|Transportation\|Hydrogen | EJ/yr | ... fuel = H2 LDV, H2 MHDV, LH2 |
| Final Energy\|Transportation\|Gases | EJ/yr | ... fuel = delivered gas |
| Energy Service\|Transportation\|Passenger | bn pkm/yr | transport service output by service |
| Energy Service\|Transportation\|Freight | bn tkm/yr | transport service output by service |
| Emissions\|CO2\|Energy\|Demand\|Transportation | Mt CO2/yr | CO2 emissions by sector (x 44/12 from MTC) |
| Price\|Carbon | US$2010/t CO2 | CO2 market price (convert from 1990$/tC) |

## D. Reproducibility
- [ ] Repository with: configuration files, every add-on XML, batch file, query file, analysis scripts, a README with run order.
- [ ] gcamdata changes as user-modification chunks or a documented diff, plus the commit hash of gcam-core.
- [ ] Output database not committed (too large); instead the queried CSVs and the scripts that make every figure.
- [ ] Each figure regenerates from one command; random seeds fixed for ensembles.
- [ ] Solver log checked: all periods solved; warnings listed.
- [ ] Data licences respected (e.g. IEA data terms) and stated.

## E. Interpretation
- [ ] Results compared with at least one other source (another model, AR6 database, national plan).
- [ ] Limits stated: no stock turnover by vehicle count, regional data generality, behavioural realism, aviation/shipping fuels.
- [ ] Uncertainty shown (ranges, ensemble or sensitivity), not single lines only.
