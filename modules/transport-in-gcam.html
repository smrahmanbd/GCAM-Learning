Go 3 mind map

Research brief

In this module you will

  • Draw GCAM's passenger transport nesting tree from service to technology
  • Build a technology's cost per passenger-km from non-fuel cost, fuel and load factor
  • Add the value of time to get a mode's generalised cost, using GCAM's 2,000-hour wage rule
  • Explain where relative-cost and absolute-cost logits are used, and how service demand responds to income and price
Spoken overview
Read the transcript

Transport in GCAM is a tree. At the top is passenger service, measured in passenger-kilometres, and freight service, in tonne-kilometres. Passenger service is shared among walking, cycling, rail, domestic aviation and road; road splits into buses and light vehicles; light vehicles split into two and three wheelers and four wheelers; and at the bottom, technologies such as petrol, hybrid, battery electric and fuel-cell cars compete. Each technology's cost per passenger-kilometre is its non-fuel cost plus its fuel cost, divided by how many people ride in the vehicle. Then GCAM adds the cost of time: an hourly wage, which is income per person divided by two thousand hours, times a multiplier for each mode, divided by the mode's speed. As people get richer, time costs more, so fast modes gain. Modes compete through relative-cost logits, and technologies within a mode through absolute-cost logits. Finally, total travel grows with income and falls with price. In version 9.1 the effective vehicle costs and intensities come from a 2023 harmonisation file, not only from the original UC Davis data.

Why this matters

For a Saudi driver in our worked example, owning and running a compact petrol car costs about $0.29 per passenger-km, of which fuel is only $0.014. The time spent driving, valued at a wage, costs almost as much as the car itself: $0.23 per passenger-km. In GCAM, cheap fuel barely moves the mode choice; speed and income do.

The nesting tree

Each demand is met by a tree of supplysectors, tranSubsectors (modes or size classes) and tranTechnologies (drivetrains). At each branch point a logit shares the market among the branches by cost. Pass-through nodes ("road", "LDV", "4W") simply hand the choice down one level.

Pitfall

Biofuels, e-fuels and ammonia are not transport technologies. Low-carbon liquids reach vehicles only through the refining sector, which blends oil, gas- and coal-to-liquids, ethanols, biodiesel and FT biofuels into one pool, refined liquids enduse. Aviation and shipping draw on the same pool as cars, so a sustainable-aviation-fuel mandate needs a model change. Core v9.1 has no power-to-liquids or DAC-to-liquids technology, and ammonia is an industrial product, not a transport fuel.

Step 1: the cost of a technology per passenger-km

Start with one vehicle. It burns I units of fuel per vehicle-km at fuel price Pf, costs N per vehicle-km to own and maintain, and carries L people on average (the load factor). The cost of one passenger-km is the cost of one vehicle-km shared among the passengers:

Pj = ( Pf · Ij + Nj ) ÷ Lj(3.3)

In the code this is TranTechnology (cvs/objects/technologies/source/tran_technology.cpp); in the XML, I is the coefficient of the fuel input, N the input-cost of the minicam-non-energy-input named "non-energy", and L the loadFactor. The non-fuel cost is built in gcamdata (zenergy_L154.transportation_UCD.R) from capital and operating costs: capital is annualised with a 10 % discount rate over 10 years and divided by the distance a vehicle travels each year.

N = [ (purchase + other + infrastructure) × CRF(10 %, 10 y) + maintenance + registration and insurance + tolls ] ÷ annual vkm(3.4)

Data: UCD_trn_data_CORE.csv (petrol car, operating costs, intensities, load factor 2.33, 14,000 vkm/yr) and OTAQ_trn_data_EMF37.csv (BEV purchase, other and charger costs), Middle East, Compact Car, 2020, v9.1. Fuel prices are illustrative round numbers, not GCAM outputs.

Worked calculation

Worked example: a compact car in the Middle East, 2020 (2005$)

  1. CRF = 0.10 × 1.110 ÷ (1.110 − 1) = 0.1627.
  2. Petrol car capital: 31,342 purchase + 10,970 other = 42,312 $; × 0.1627 = 6,886 $/yr.
  3. Operating: 448 maintenance + 1,550 registration and insurance = 1,998 $/yr.
  4. Non-fuel per vehicle-km: (6,886 + 1,998) ÷ 14,000 km = 0.635 $/vkm.
  5. Fuel: 3.32 MJ/vkm × 10 $/GJ (illustrative) = 0.0332 $/vkm.
  6. Per passenger-km: (0.635 + 0.0332) ÷ 2.33 persons = 0.286 $/pkm.
  7. Battery car, same steps (capital 51,991 $ incl. 1,409 $ charger; 1.03 MJ/vkm at 25 $/GJ): 0.328 $/pkm.

Answer: the BEV is 15 % dearer per pkm in 2020; capital is 74 % of the petrol car's cost and fuel only 5 %

Research note

Which file sets the cost you think you are changing? For cars, trucks, buses, rail, aviation and ships, gcamdata overwrites the UC Davis (UCD) values with OTAQ_trn_data_EMF37.csv: "MODIFIED intensity and capital cost … based on AUTONOMIE and TEMPO harmonization" (source workbook dated 14 March 2023). In our example the petrol car's costs still come from UCD (no OTAQ row), but the BEV's come from OTAQ. Edit the wrong file and nothing changes.

Step 2: the generalised cost of a mode

A traveller also pays with time. GCAM converts income into an hourly wage, W = GDP per person ÷ 2,000 hours (40 hours × 50 weeks, hard-coded in TranSubsector::getTimeValue), scales it by a time-value multiplier V and divides by the mode's door-to-door speed S:

Pi = Σj sji Pji + W · Vi ÷ Si,    W = GDP per capita ÷ (40 h × 50 wk)(3.5)

The time term is added only where addTimeValue = 1: on passenger modes and on the 4W node (once, at the LDV-versus-other-modes level), never on freight or on drivetrain choice. Since v8.5 the GDP used here is deflated from 1990 to 1975 dollars inside the code.

Worked calculation

Worked example: the price of time

  1. GDP per person $20,000 (illustrative) ÷ 2,000 h = W = $10 per hour.
  2. Car: 10 × 0.82 ÷ 35 km/h = 0.234 $/pkm.
  3. Bus: 10 × 0.808 ÷ 21 km/h = 0.385 $/pkm — slower, so time costs more than the bus fare.
  4. Rail: 10 × 0.80 ÷ 42 km/h = 0.190 $/pkm.
  5. Double the income and every time cost doubles; fast modes gain on slow ones.

Answer: income growth pushes travellers towards faster modes even if money costs stay the same

Step 3: sharing — two kinds of logit

At every branch, shares follow a logit. GCAM has two forms (documentation, Economic Choice). The relative-cost logit compares cost ratios; the absolute-cost logit compares cost differences, scaled by a base value p0:

relative: si = αi Piγ ÷ Σk αk Pkγ     absolute: si = αi eγ Pi/p0 ÷ Σk αk eγ Pk/p0(3.6)

Which form applies where? In the gcamdata files every tranSubsector (choice among drivetrains) is set to absolute-cost-logit, as is the trn_pass_road_LDV_4W sector. The other supplysectors leave logit.type empty, and gcamdata then fills in the default, the relative-cost logit (add_logit_tables_xml in R/xml.R). So mode choice is relative, drivetrain choice is absolute. The absolute form copes with costs that can be near zero or negative, which matters once carbon prices and subsidies enter.

Worked calculation

Worked example: calibrating a share-weight

  1. Observed 2021: BEVs take 1 % of new car pkm, though they cost 15 % more (pBEV = 1.15 p0).
  2. With αICE = 1 and γ = −8: sBEV/sICE = αBEV · e−8(1.15 − 1) = αBEV · e−1.2.
  3. Set the ratio to 0.01/0.99 = 0.0101: αBEV = 0.0101 ÷ 0.3012 = 0.0335.
  4. Check: share = 0.010, the observed 1 %.
  5. Now the future path of αBEV (0.034 → 1 by 2030 in the s-curve) does as much work as any cost change.

Answer: αBEV ≈ 0.034; the share-weight hides everything costs do not explain

Step 4: how much travel

The total service demand grows with income and population and falls with the aggregate price of the service (the average cost that the nest passes up). In minicam_price_elasticity_function.cpp:

Dt = Dt−1 · (Yt/Yt−1)α · (Pt/Pt−1)β · (Nt/Nt−1)(3.7)

Y is GDP per person, P the service price, N population (only for per-capita demands); α and β are the income and price elasticities in the table above. Because α = 1 for passenger travel, travel per person grows as fast as income unless prices rise.

In GCAM

Where it lives (v9.1). Chunks: input/gcamdata/R/zenergy_L154.transportation_UCD.R (calibration, costs, intensities by UCD region) and zenergy_L254.transportation_UCD.R (level-2 tables such as L254.StubTranTechCost, L254.StubTranTechLoadFactor, L254.tranSubsectorVOTT, L254.IncomeElasticity_trn); XML writer zenergy_xml_transportation_UCD_CORE.R → transportation_UCD_CORE.xml, _SSP1, _SSP3, _SSP5. Assumptions: A54.*_revised.csv (the _revised files are the ones used), UCD_trn_data_*.csv, OTAQ_trn_data_EMF37.csv, mappings UCD_techs_revised.csv, UCD_ctry.csv.

Calibration. IEA transport energy by country and fuel is split into UCD modes, size classes and technologies with 2005 UCD shares, scaled to GCAM end-use energy, converted to vkm (÷ intensity) and pkm (× load factor); share-weights then reproduce each base year to 2021. Parameters exist for 14 UCD regions and are mapped to GCAM's 32: Saudi values are generic "Middle East" values.

Pitfall

Two documentation pages disagree with the code. en_technologies.html still gives 1990$ and BTU per vehicle-km (v8.2 units). And the docs' general advice that passenger transport suits the relative-cost logit is true for mode choice but not for drivetrain choice, which the input files set to the absolute-cost form. Always check the XML you are running.

def interp(points, year):
    """Linear interpolation in a {year: value} dict, flat outside the range."""
    ys = sorted(points)
    if year <= ys[0]:
        return points[ys[0]]
    if year >= ys[-1]:
        return points[ys[-1]]
    for a, b in zip(ys, ys[1:]):
        if a <= year <= b:
            return points[a] + (points[b] - points[a]) * (year - a) / (b - a)

        "BEV":     dict(fuel="elect_td_trn", nf=interp({2020: 0.14, 2050: 0.11}, year), mj=0.30, lf=1.3,
                        sw={2020: 0, 2025: 0, 2030: 1}),
    }
    rail = {"Electric": dict(fuel="elect_td_trn", nf=16.005, mj=53.197, lf=1152.0)}
    air = {"Liquids": dict(fuel="refined liquids enduse", nf=13.77, mj=interp({2020: 366.5, 2050: 310.6}, year),
                           lf=interp({2020: 148.8, 2050: 161.6}, year))}
    return {"Car": car, "Bus": bus, "2W and 3W": two_w, "Passenger Rail": rail, "Domestic Aviation": air}


TECH_EXP = {"Car": -8, "Bus": -3, "2W and 3W": -8, "Passenger Rail": -1, "Domestic Aviation": -6}
# mode-level settings: time-value multiplier, addTimeValue, speed (km/h)
MODES = {
    "Walk":              dict(vott=1.066, add=1, speed=lambda y: 4.8),
    "Cycle":             dict(vott=1.066, add=1, speed=lambda y: 15.0),
    "Passenger Rail":    dict(vott=0.80, add=1, speed=lambda y: 42.0),
    "Domestic Aviation": dict(vott=1.244, add=1, speed=lambda y: 197.0),

    "trn_pass":          (-2, ["Walk", "Cycle", "Passenger Rail", "Domestic Aviation", "road"]),

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- logit functions
def rel_shares(prices, sws, g):
    w = {k: (sws[k] * prices[k] ** g if sws[k] > 0 else 0.0) for k in prices}
    t = sum(w.values())
    return {k: v / t for k, v in w.items()}


def abs_shares(costs, sws, beta, p0):
    w = {k: (sws[k] * math.exp(beta * costs[k] / p0) if sws[k] > 0 else 0.0) for k in costs}
    t = sum(w.values())
    return {k: v / t for k, v in w.items()}


def run(name, sc):
    cal = {}                     # calibrated share-weights and base costs (from the base year)
    rows, demand_prev, price_prev = [], None, None
    for year in YEARS:
        T = techs(year, sc)
        wage = gdp_pc(year, sc) * 1000 / (40 * 50)        # 2005$ per hour
        tech_cost, tech_share, mode_money = {}, {}, {}
        # 1) technology level (absolute-cost logit inside each mode)
        for mode, tt in T.items():
            costs = {k: (v["nf"] + fuel_price(v["fuel"], year, sc) * v["mj"] / 1000) / v["lf"] for k, v in tt.items()}
            tech_cost[mode] = costs
            if year == BASE:
                base = BASE_PKM[mode] if isinstance(BASE_PKM[mode], dict) else {list(tt)[0]: BASE_PKM[mode]}
                calk = {k: base.get(k, 0.0) for k in tt}
                tot = sum(calk.values())
                p0 = max(costs[k] for k in tt if calk[k] > 0)            # most expensive calibrated option
                anchor = max(calk, key=calk.get)
                sws = {}
                for k in tt:
                    if calk[k] > 0:   # inverse of the absolute logit (calibrate_share_weight_visitor.cpp)
                        sws[k] = (calk[k] / tot) / (calk[anchor] / tot) * math.exp(TECH_EXP[mode] / p0 * (costs[anchor] - costs[k]))
                    else:
                        sws[k] = 0.0
                cal[mode] = dict(p0=p0, sw=sws)
            sws = {k: (interp(v["sw"], year) if "sw" in v and cal[mode]["sw"][k] == 0 else cal[mode]["sw"][k]) for k, v in tt.items()}
            sh = abs_shares(costs, sws, TECH_EXP[mode], cal[mode]["p0"])
            tech_share[mode] = sh
            mode_money[mode] = sum(sh[k] * costs[k] for k in costs)
        mode_money["Walk"] = mode_money["Cycle"] = 0.0      # non-motorised: no money cost
        mode_money["4W"] = mode_money["Car"]                # pass-through: 4W sector holds only Car

        def mode_price(m):
            md = MODES[m]
            tv = wage * md["vott"] / md["speed"](year) if md["add"] else 0.0
            return mode_money[m] + tv

        # 2) nests from the bottom up (relative-cost logit among modes)
        sector_price, mode_sh = {}, {}
        for sector in ["trn_pass_road_LDV", "trn_pass_road", "trn_pass"]:
            g, kids = NEST[sector]
            prices = {}
            for k in kids:
                prices[k] = sector_price[CHILD_SECTOR[k]] if k in ("road", "LDV") else mode_price(k)
            if year == BASE:
                def base_out(k):
                    if k == "road": return base_out("Bus") + base_out("LDV")
                    if k == "LDV": return base_out("2W and 3W") + base_out("4W")
                    if k == "4W": return sum(BASE_PKM["Car"].values())
                    v = BASE_PKM[k]
                    return sum(v.values()) if isinstance(v, dict) else v
                outs = {k: base_out(k) for k in kids}
                anchor = max(outs, key=outs.get)
                cal[sector] = {k: (outs[k] / outs[anchor]) * (prices[anchor] / prices[k]) ** g for k in kids}
            sh = rel_shares(prices, cal[sector], g)
            mode_sh[sector] = sh
            sector_price[sector] = sum(sh[k] * prices[k] for k in kids)
        P = sector_price["trn_pass"]

        # 3) total passenger demand (billion pkm)
        if year == BASE:
            D = sum(BASE_PKM[m] if not isinstance(BASE_PKM[m], dict) else sum(BASE_PKM[m].values()) for m in BASE_PKM)
        else:
            y_ratio = gdp_pc(year, sc) / gdp_pc(year - 5, sc)
            D = demand_prev * y_ratio ** 1.0 * (P / price_prev) ** -1.25 * population(year) / population(year - 5)
        demand_prev, price_prev = D, P

        # 4) pkm by mode and technology, energy and CO2
        top, road, ldv = mode_sh["trn_pass"], mode_sh["trn_pass_road"], mode_sh["trn_pass_road_LDV"]
        mode_pkm = {"Walk": D * top["Walk"], "Cycle": D * top["Cycle"], "Passenger Rail": D * top["Passenger Rail"],
                    "Domestic Aviation": D * top["Domestic Aviation"], "Bus": D * top["road"] * road["Bus"],
                    "2W and 3W": D * top["road"] * road["LDV"] * ldv["2W and 3W"],
                    "Car": D * top["road"] * road["LDV"] * ldv["4W"]}
        energy, co2 = {}, 0.0
        for m, pk in mode_pkm.items():
            rows.append((name, year, "service output by mode", m, pk, "billion pkm"))
            rows.append((name, year, "mode share", m, pk / D, "fraction"))
            if m in T:
                for k, s in tech_share[m].items():
                    v = T[m][k]
                    ej = pk * s / v["lf"] * v["mj"] / 1000              # billion vkm x MJ/vkm = PJ -> EJ
                    energy[v["fuel"]] = energy.get(v["fuel"], 0.0) + ej
                    co2 += ej * 1e3 * EF[v["fuel"]]                      # EJ -> PJ = million GJ; x t/GJ = Mt
                    rows.append((name, year, "tech share", f"{m}|{k}", s, "fraction"))
        rows.append((name, year, "service output", "trn_pass", D, "billion pkm"))
        rows.append((name, year, "service price", "trn_pass", P, "2005$/pkm"))
        for f, e in sorted(energy.items()):
            rows.append((name, year, "final energy by fuel", f, e, "EJ"))
        rows.append((name, year, "CO2 (direct)", "trn_pass", co2, "MtCO2"))
    return rows

# gcam_transport_toy.R -- R twin of gcam_transport_toy.py (same structure, same numbers).
#
# A stand-alone passenger-transport model that mimics GCAM 9.1's transport structure:
#   nests trn_pass -> road -> trn_pass_road -> LDV -> trn_pass_road_LDV -> 4W -> Car;
#   relative-cost logit among modes (trn_pass -2, trn_pass_road -4, trn_pass_road_LDV -4; A54.sector.csv),
#   absolute-cost logit among technologies (Car -8, 2W and 3W -8, Bus -3, Passenger Rail -1,
#   Domestic Aviation -6; A54.tranSubsector_logit_revised.csv);
#   cost per pkm = (non-fuel $/vkm + fuel price x intensity) / load factor (tran_technology.cpp);
#   time value = GDP per capita / 2000 h x time-value-multiplier / speed (tran_subsector.cpp 175-214);
#   demand elasticities income 1.0, price -1.25 (A54.demand.csv);
#   share-weights calibrated to the 2020 base year (calibrate_share_weight_visitor.cpp).
# See the header of gcam_transport_toy.py for sources, units and simplifications.
# Numbers are illustrative (2005$). Run: Rscript gcam_transport_toy.R

YEARS <- seq(2020, 2100, 5); BASE <- 2020

interp <- function(points, year) {           # points: named numeric vector, names = years
  ys <- as.numeric(names(points)); v <- unname(points); o <- order(ys); ys <- ys[o]; v <- v[o]
  if (year <= ys[1]) return(v[1]); if (year >= ys[length(ys)]) return(v[length(v)])
  approx(ys, v, xout = year)$y
}
pts <- function(...) { x <- c(...); x }      # pts(`2020` = 1, `2050` = 2)

EF <- c("refined liquids enduse" = 19.6 * 44 / 12 / 1000, "delivered gas" = 14.2 * 44 / 12 / 1000,
        "elect_td_trn" = 0, "H2 LDV" = 0, "H2 MHDV" = 0)

SCENARIOS <- list(
  "Reference"    = list(),
  "BEV-low"      = list(bev_cost_mult = 0.70),
  "Carbon-price" = list(carbon = TRUE),
  "Fast-wage"    = list(gdp_growth = 0.03),
  "LF-up"        = list(car_lf_2050 = 2.60))

opt <- function(sc, key, default) if (is.null(sc[[key]])) default else sc[[key]]

fuel_price <- function(fuel, year, sc) {
  base <- switch(fuel,
    "refined liquids enduse" = interp(pts(`2020` = 15, `2050` = 18), year),
    "delivered gas" = 8, "elect_td_trn" = 30,
    "H2 LDV" = interp(pts(`2020` = 45, `2050` = 30), year),
    "H2 MHDV" = interp(pts(`2020` = 40, `2050` = 28), year))
  if (isTRUE(sc$carbon) && year >= 2025) base <- base + EF[[fuel]] * 50 * 1.05^(year - 2025)
  base
}
gdp_pc <- function(year, sc) 18 * (1 + opt(sc, "gdp_growth", 0.02))^(year - BASE)
population <- function(year) 260 + 140 * (1 - exp(-0.025 * (year - BASE)))

tech <- function(fuel, nf, mj, lf, sw = NULL) list(fuel = fuel, nf = nf, mj = mj, lf = lf, sw = sw)
techs <- function(year, sc) {
  car_lf <- interp(c(`2020` = 2.33, `2050` = opt(sc, "car_lf_2050", 2.00)), year)
  bev_mult <- if (year >= 2030) opt(sc, "bev_cost_mult", 1.0) else 1.0
  list(
    "Car" = list(
      "Liquids" = tech("refined liquids enduse", interp(pts(`2020` = .6345, `2030` = .6747, `2050` = .7652), year),
                       interp(pts(`2020` = 3.322, `2030` = 3.101, `2050` = 2.718), year), car_lf),
      "Hybrid Liquids" = tech("refined liquids enduse", interp(pts(`2020` = .6629, `2030` = .7081, `2050` = .7872), year),
                       interp(pts(`2020` = 2.581, `2030` = 2.454, `2050` = 2.226), year), car_lf),
      "NG" = tech("delivered gas", interp(pts(`2020` = .6599, `2030` = .7017, `2050` = .7958), year),
                  interp(pts(`2020` = 3.355, `2030` = 3.132, `2050` = 2.745), year), car_lf),
      "BEV" = tech("elect_td_trn", bev_mult * interp(pts(`2020` = .7407, `2030` = .6894, `2050` = .7652), year),
                   interp(pts(`2020` = 1.026, `2030` = 1.019, `2050` = 1.005), year), car_lf,
                   pts(`2020` = 0, `2025` = 0, `2030` = 1)),
      "FCEV" = tech("H2 LDV", interp(pts(`2020` = .8050, `2030` = .7280, `2050` = .7799), year),
                    interp(pts(`2020` = 1.572, `2030` = 1.518, `2050` = 1.418), year), car_lf,
                    pts(`2020` = 0, `2030` = 0, `2050` = 0.5))),
    "Bus" = list(
      "Liquids" = tech("refined liquids enduse", 1.6319, interp(pts(`2020` = 7.908, `2050` = 7.884), year), 25),
      "NG" = tech("delivered gas", 1.7609, interp(pts(`2020` = 7.908, `2050` = 7.884), year), 25),
      "BEV" = tech("elect_td_trn", interp(pts(`2020` = 2.00, `2050` = 1.70), year), 3.0, 25,
                   pts(`2020` = 0, `2025` = 0, `2050` = 1))),
    "2W and 3W" = list(
      "Liquids" = tech("refined liquids enduse", 0.12, 1.0, 1.3),
      "BEV" = tech("elect_td_trn", interp(pts(`2020` = 0.14, `2050` = 0.11), year), 0.30, 1.3,
                   pts(`2020` = 0, `2025` = 0, `2030` = 1))),
    "Passenger Rail" = list("Electric" = tech("elect_td_trn", 16.005, 53.197, 1152)),
    "Domestic Aviation" = list("Liquids" = tech("refined liquids enduse", 13.77,
                               interp(pts(`2020` = 366.5, `2050` = 310.6), year),
                               interp(pts(`2020` = 148.8, `2050` = 161.6), year))))
}

TECH_EXP <- c("Car" = -8, "Bus" = -3, "2W and 3W" = -8, "Passenger Rail" = -1, "Domestic Aviation" = -6)
MODES <- list(
  "Walk" = list(vott = 1.066, add = 1, speed = function(y) 4.8),
  "Cycle" = list(vott = 1.066, add = 1, speed = function(y) 15),
  "Passenger Rail" = list(vott = 0.80, add = 1, speed = function(y) 42),
  "Domestic Aviation" = list(vott = 1.244, add = 1, speed = function(y) 197),
  "Bus" = list(vott = 0.808, add = 1, speed = function(y) interp(pts(`2020` = 22, `2050` = 24, `2100` = 25), y)),
  "2W and 3W" = list(vott = 0.82, add = 1, speed = function(y) 30),
  "4W" = list(vott = 0.82, add = 1, speed = function(y) interp(pts(`2020` = 35, `2050` = 40, `2100` = 42), y)))
NEST <- list(
  "trn_pass_road_LDV" = list(g = -4, kids = c("2W and 3W", "4W")),
  "trn_pass_road" = list(g = -4, kids = c("Bus", "LDV")),
  "trn_pass" = list(g = -2, kids = c("Walk", "Cycle", "Passenger Rail", "Domestic Aviation", "road")))
CHILD_SECTOR <- c("road" = "trn_pass_road", "LDV" = "trn_pass_road_LDV")
BASE_PKM <- list("Walk" = 70, "Cycle" = 25, "Passenger Rail" = 6, "Domestic Aviation" = 55,
                 "Bus" = c("Liquids" = 157, "NG" = 3), "2W and 3W" = c("Liquids" = 45),
                 "Car" = c("Liquids" = 1120, "Hybrid Liquids" = 20, "NG" = 10))

rel_shares <- function(prices, sws, g) { w <- ifelse(sws > 0, sws * prices^g, 0); w / sum(w) }
abs_shares <- function(costs, sws, beta, p0) { w <- ifelse(sws > 0, sws * exp(beta * costs / p0), 0); w / sum(w) }
base_out <- function(k) {
  if (k == "road") return(base_out("Bus") + base_out("LDV"))
  if (k == "LDV") return(base_out("2W and 3W") + base_out("4W"))
  if (k == "4W") return(sum(BASE_PKM[["Car"]]))
  sum(BASE_PKM[[k]])
}

run <- function(name, sc) {
  cal <- list(); rows <- list(); D_prev <- NA; P_prev <- NA
  add <- function(year, var, item, value, unit)
    rows[[length(rows) + 1]] <<- data.frame(scenario = name, year = year, variable = var, item = item, value = value, unit = unit)
  for (year in YEARS) {
    Tt <- techs(year, sc); wage <- gdp_pc(year, sc) * 1000 / (40 * 50)
    tech_share <- list(); mode_money <- c()
    for (mode in names(Tt)) {
      tt <- Tt[[mode]]
      costs <- sapply(tt, function(v) (v$nf + fuel_price(v$fuel, year, sc) * v$mj / 1000) / v$lf)
      if (year == BASE) {
        b <- BASE_PKM[[mode]]; if (is.null(names(b))) names(b) <- names(tt)[1]
        calk <- sapply(names(tt), function(k) if (k %in% names(b)) b[[k]] else 0)
        tot <- sum(calk); p0 <- max(costs[calk > 0]); anchor <- names(which.max(calk))
        sws <- sapply(names(tt), function(k) if (calk[[k]] > 0)
          (calk[[k]] / tot) / (calk[[anchor]] / tot) * exp(TECH_EXP[[mode]] / p0 * (costs[[anchor]] - costs[[k]])) else 0)
        cal[[mode]] <- list(p0 = p0, sw = sws)
      }
      sws <- sapply(names(tt), function(k) if (!is.null(tt[[k]]$sw) && cal[[mode]]$sw[[k]] == 0) interp(tt[[k]]$sw, year) else cal[[mode]]$sw[[k]])
      sh <- abs_shares(costs, sws, TECH_EXP[[mode]], cal[[mode]]$p0)
      tech_share[[mode]] <- sh; mode_money[mode] <- sum(sh * costs)
    }
    mode_money["Walk"] <- 0; mode_money["Cycle"] <- 0; mode_money["4W"] <- mode_money[["Car"]]
    mode_price <- function(m) { md <- MODES[[m]]; mode_money[[m]] + if (md$add == 1) wage * md$vott / md$speed(year) else 0 }
    sector_price <- c(); mode_sh <- list()
    for (sector in names(NEST)) {
      g <- NEST[[sector]]$g; kids <- NEST[[sector]]$kids
      prices <- sapply(kids, function(k) if (k %in% names(CHILD_SECTOR)) sector_price[[CHILD_SECTOR[[k]]]] else mode_price(k))
      if (year == BASE) {
        outs <- sapply(kids, base_out); anchor <- names(which.max(outs))
        cal[[sector]] <- (outs / outs[[anchor]]) * (prices[[anchor]] / prices)^g
      }
      sh <- rel_shares(prices, cal[[sector]], g); mode_sh[[sector]] <- sh
      sector_price[sector] <- sum(sh * prices)
    }
    P <- sector_price[["trn_pass"]]
    if (year == BASE) D <- sum(sapply(BASE_PKM, sum)) else
      D <- D_prev * (gdp_pc(year, sc) / gdp_pc(year - 5, sc))^1.0 * (P / P_prev)^-1.25 * population(year) / population(year - 5)
    D_prev <- D; P_prev <- P
    top <- mode_sh[["trn_pass"]]; road <- mode_sh[["trn_pass_road"]]; ldv <- mode_sh[["trn_pass_road_LDV"]]
    mode_pkm <- c("Walk" = D * top[["Walk"]], "Cycle" = D * top[["Cycle"]], "Passenger Rail" = D * top[["Passenger Rail"]],
                  "Domestic Aviation" = D * top[["Domestic Aviation"]], "Bus" = D * top[["road"]] * road[["Bus"]],
                  "2W and 3W" = D * top[["road"]] * road[["LDV"]] * ldv[["2W and 3W"]],
                  "Car" = D * top[["road"]] * road[["LDV"]] * ldv[["4W"]])
    energy <- c(); co2 <- 0
    for (m in names(mode_pkm)) {
      pk <- mode_pkm[[m]]
      add(year, "service output by mode", m, pk, "billion pkm"); add(year, "mode share", m, pk / D, "fraction")
      if (m %in% names(Tt)) for (k in names(tech_share[[m]])) {
        v <- Tt[[m]][[k]]; s <- tech_share[[m]][[k]]
        ej <- pk * s / v$lf * v$mj / 1000
        energy[v$fuel] <- (if (v$fuel %in% names(energy)) energy[[v$fuel]] else 0) + ej
        co2 <- co2 + ej * 1e3 * EF[[v$fuel]]
        add(year, "tech share", paste0(m, "|", k), s, "fraction")
      }
    }
    add(year, "service output", "trn_pass", D, "billion pkm"); add(year, "service price", "trn_pass", P, "2005$/pkm")
    for (f in sort(names(energy))) add(year, "final energy by fuel", f, energy[[f]], "EJ")
    add(year, "CO2 (direct)", "trn_pass", co2, "MtCO2")
  }
  do.call(rbind, rows)
}

Research notes

  • Passenger transport is a tree: service → modes → road → light vehicles → 4W → technologies
  • Technology cost per pkm = (non-fuel cost + fuel price × intensity) ÷ load factor
  • Mode cost adds wage × time-value multiplier ÷ speed, with wage = GDP per person ÷ 2,000 h
  • Modes compete by relative-cost logits; technologies inside a mode by absolute-cost logits
  • Edit OTAQ_trn_data_EMF37.csv, not only the UC Davis data, when changing most vehicle costs

Check yourself

1. GCAM's hourly wage for the value of time is…

TranSubsector uses GDP per capita over 40 hours × 50 weeks.

2. Car occupancy (load factor) rises from 1.5 to 2.0. The cost per passenger-km…

Cost per vkm is divided by people per vehicle: 1.5/2.0 = 0.75.

3. Which logit type is used for technologies inside the Car mode?

A54.tranSubsector_logit_revised.csv sets absolute-cost logits within modes (Car −8).

4. Why do faster modes gain share as countries get richer, even with unchanged prices?

The time term grows with the wage, penalising slow modes.

5. Where do most vehicle costs and intensities in GCAM 9.1 come from?

gcamdata overwrites UCD values with the 2023 EMF37/Autonomie/TEMPO harmonisation for most modes.

Reading list

New words

Load factor
People (or tonnes) carried per vehicle; divides the vehicle's cost and energy per km.
Value of time
Wage × time-value multiplier ÷ speed: the cost of the hours spent travelling.
Generalised cost
Money cost plus time cost of travelling one passenger-km by a mode.
Absolute-cost logit
A logit on cost differences (exp of cost), used for technologies within a mode.
Service demand
Total passenger-km or tonne-km demanded, driven by income and price elasticities.
UCD database
The University of California, Davis transport database behind GCAM's base transport data.
