Go 3 · Research

Research with GCAM: transport

Design, build and defend a research study with GCAM, using the transport sector as the running case.

Start module 3.1Go 3 handbook (PDF)Zooming mind mapGo 3 code kit (zip)

Is this go for you?

You need: Go 2, or its exit check. A GCAM 9.x installation for the implementation steps, plus R or Python.

How it works: Each module reads like a research brief: the question, the method, the implementation in GCAM's XML and code, the evidence from the literature, and the pitfalls.

Quick entry check

1. Passenger mode choice in GCAM includes…

The generalised cost adds wage-based time cost to money cost.

2. An add-on XML file…

Add-ons override the base inputs they name; load order matters.

3. Scenario discovery finds…

It searches an ensemble for the input ranges that explain, say, high emissions.

All three right? You are ready for the research modules. Not sure? Revisit Go 2.

What you will produce

  • A research proposal with a scenario design matrix and an uncertainty plan
  • Add-on XML scenarios for transport technologies and policies, run as a batch
  • A reproducible analysis: decomposition, sensitivity and scenario discovery
  • A short paper and talk following the GCAM community guidelines

The path

  1. 3.1GCAM as research softwareTreat GCAM as research software: know the pipeline from source data to results, how the solver works, what calibration fixes, and what you must keep to rebuild any run.45 min
    Foundations
  2. 3.2Transport in GCAM, in fullPassenger and freight transport in GCAM 9.1 in full: the nesting tree, the cost per passenger-km, the value of time, two kinds of logit, and how much travel is demanded.60 min
    Model detail
  3. 3.3What the literature knowsA structured review of what GCAM and other integrated assessment models say about decarbonising transport: foundations, model comparisons, applications by mode and fuel, and Saudi Arabia.50 min
    Evidence
  4. 3.4Designing a studyTurn a topic into a research question GCAM can answer, design a Reference and levers, choose price or quantity policies, and plan the uncertainty analysis before you run anything.45 min
    Method
  5. 3.5Building transport scenariosBuild transport scenarios as GCAM add-on XML: costs, share-weights with interpolation rules, load factors and speeds, carbon taxes, net-zero constraints and a zero-emission-vehicle credit market, run as a batch.60 min
    Implementation
  6. 3.6Analysing and stress-testing resultsGet results out the same way every time, compare against Reference, decompose changes in transport CO2, stress-test with sensitivity runs and scenario discovery, and benchmark against other models.55 min
    Analysis
  7. 3.7Going finer and widerGo finer by giving a country its own region (the GCAM-KSA approach) or downscaling regional results to countries, and go wider by coupling GCAM with vehicle-stock and national energy models.45 min
    Scale
  8. 3.8Limits, ethics and writing it upKnow what GCAM transport cannot see, weigh whose future and whose cost your scenarios assume, describe GCAM properly in a paper, report in IAMC format, and publish a reproducible package.40 min
    Write-up

An open course on the Global Change Analysis Model (GCAM), taught in three goes. GCAM is developed by the Joint Global Change Research Institute (PNNL); this is independent teaching material. GCAM facts follow the GCAM v8.2 documentation and the gcam-core v9.1 source; explorers and toy models are teaching versions with illustrative numbers.
