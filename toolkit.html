An open course on the Global Change Analysis Model (GCAM), taught in three goes. GCAM is developed by the Joint Global Change Research Institute (PNNL); this is independent teaching material. GCAM facts follow the GCAM v8.2 documentation and the gcam-core v9.1 source; explorers and toy models are teaching versions with illustrative numbers.
