Learn GCAM three times, each time deeper

GCAM links people, energy, land, water and climate in one model of the world. You will meet the whole system three times: first as a picture, then as working parts that push on each other, then as a research tool you change and test.

Go 1Introductory Foundations of Integrated Assessment ModellingUnderstand what GCAM and other integrated assessment models are for, what goes in, what comes out, and how to read a scenario. 6 modules · about 3.5 hours · you need: nothingGo 2Intermediate GCAM Architecture and System InteractionsOpen each GCAM component, see the rule it follows and the numbers it needs, and trace how it pushes on the others. 8 modules · about 6.0 hours · you need: go 1, or its exit checkGo 3Advanced Applied Research with GCAM: Transport SystemsDesign, build and defend a research study with GCAM, using the transport sector as the running case. 8 modules · about 6.7 hours · you need: go 2, or its exit check

How the goes fit together

Start where you are

Each go opens with a three-question check. If you already know the answers, skip ahead. Each go closes with an exit check: tick off what you can do before moving on.

Each go works differently

Go 1 is pictures and predict-then-check explorers. Go 2 is connection maps and small models you build. Go 3 is a research brief for each step of a real study, with GCAM files and code.

Real model, honest toys

GCAM facts follow the v8.2 documentation and the gcam-core v9.1 source. Explorers and toy models are small teaching versions; their numbers are illustrative and say so.
